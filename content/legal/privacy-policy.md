# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: 01 January 2027**

**Zusammen Tours and Travel Agency (MICE Department)** ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a proposal, subscribe to our communications, or otherwise interact with us.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

- Full name
- Company or organisation name
- Job title or position
- Email address
- Telephone or mobile number
- Country and city
- Event or conference requirements
- Preferred event dates
- Number of delegates or participants
- Accommodation requirements
- Venue and meeting requirements
- Travel and transportation requirements
- Budget information
- Any other information you choose to provide when contacting us

We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and general website usage information.

## 2. How We Collect Information

We may collect information when you:

- Complete a contact or enquiry form;
- Request a proposal or quotation;
- Subscribe to our newsletter or updates;
- Contact us by email or telephone;
- Communicate with us regarding a meeting, incentive, conference or exhibition; or
- Browse or interact with our website.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

- Respond to enquiries and requests;
- Prepare proposals and quotations;
- Plan and coordinate meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions;
- Arrange venues, accommodation, transportation, activities and related services;
- Communicate with you regarding your event;
- Provide customer support;
- Send newsletters and relevant updates where you have subscribed;
- Improve our website and services;
- Maintain business records;
- Prevent fraud, misuse or security incidents; and
- Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected, except where permitted or required by law.

## 4. Sharing of Personal Information

To deliver our services, we may share relevant information with trusted service providers and business partners, including:

- Hotels and accommodation providers;
- Conference and event venues;
- Transportation and transfer providers;
- Airlines and travel-service providers;
- Tour operators and activity providers;
- Event suppliers and contractors;
- Technology and website service providers; and
- Other suppliers involved in delivering your requested event or service.

We will only share information that is reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

We may also disclose personal information where required by law, court order, governmental authority or other lawful process.

## 5. International Transfers

Our services may involve international delegates, suppliers and service providers. As a result, personal information may sometimes be shared with service providers or business partners outside Namibia.

Where this occurs, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is handled appropriately and only for legitimate business purposes.

## 6. Marketing Communications

If you subscribe to our marketing communications, we may send you:

- Industry updates;
- Destination information;
- Conference and event information;
- Incentive travel information;
- Special offers;
- New service announcements; and
- Other information relevant to business travel and events.

You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in the communication or by contacting us directly.

## 7. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality, understand website traffic and enhance your browsing experience.

You can adjust your browser settings to refuse or delete cookies. Disabling certain cookies may, however, affect some website functionality.

Where third-party analytics or other services are used on our website, those providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes and complying with legal, accounting or regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy or anonymise it where appropriate.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Ask what personal information we hold about you;
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- Request deletion of information where there is no legitimate reason for us to retain it;
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- Opt out of marketing communications; and
- Raise a concern about how your personal information is being handled.

Privacy-related requests can be submitted using the contact details below.

## 11. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including hotels, venues, tourism businesses, suppliers, social-media platforms and other service providers.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are primarily intended for businesses, organisations, event organisers and adult travellers.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing purposes.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality, legal requirements or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.
