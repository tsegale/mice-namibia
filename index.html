09 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from organisations planning MICE experiences across Africa.

What does MICE stand for?

MICE stands for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions. It covers corporate events, business meetings, incentive travel, conferences and related group programmes.

Which African destinations do you offer for MICE travel?

We offer MICE programmes across Botswana, Kenya, Namibia, Rwanda, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

Can you create a tailor-made MICE programme?

Yes. We design customised programmes based on your group size, objectives, budget, preferred destination, dates and interests.

Can business events be combined with leisure or safari experiences?

Absolutely. Africa offers excellent opportunities to combine meetings and conferences with safaris, wildlife, cultural experiences, adventure and leisure extensions.

How do we request a MICE proposal?

Contact us with your destination, preferred dates, number of delegates, type of event, accommodation requirements and any activities you would like to include. We will use this information to develop a tailor-made proposal for your group.
