About This Venue

The WICC (Windhoek International Convention Centre) is built for larger conferences, exhibitions and trade shows, with adaptable event floors capable of hosting keynote sessions, breakout meetings and exhibition stands under one roof.

Its central Windhoek location and dedicated event infrastructure make it a natural fit for organisations hosting multi-day conferences or large-scale corporate gatherings.
