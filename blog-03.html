Top 10 Conference & Meeting Venues in Namibia

Venues10 min readZusammen Travels MICE

From large-scale international conferences in Windhoek to executive retreats on the coast, these are 10 conference and meeting venues worth considering for your next event in Namibia.

Choosing the right venue can make the difference between a conference that simply works and one that leaves a lasting impression.

Namibia is becoming an increasingly attractive destination for meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions (MICE), offering planners a combination of professional venues, quality accommodation, spectacular landscapes and distinctive African experiences.

Whether you are organising a large international conference, corporate meeting, executive retreat, association event, incentive programme or private business gathering, Namibia offers options across Windhoek, Swakopmund, Walvis Bay and beyond.

We have selected 10 venues that stand out for their capacity, location, facilities, accommodation, flexibility and overall suitability for business events.

The right venue does more than accommodate your delegates. It creates the environment in which your event succeeds.

1. Windhoek International Convention Centre (WICC): Windhoek

Windhoek International Convention Centre (WICC)

Best for: Large conferences, conventions, exhibitions and international events

If you are planning a large-scale conference in Namibia, Windhoek International Convention Centre (WICC) is one of the most obvious places to start.

WICC is Namibia's largest conference venue and offers 15 adaptable venues, making it suitable for everything from major conventions and exhibitions to smaller corporate meetings.

Its flagship Grand Ballroom spans approximately 1,565 square metres and can accommodate up to 2,400 delegates in theatre style, 1,800 in conference style and 1,200 for banquets, depending on the configuration. The ballroom can also be divided into smaller spaces, providing flexibility for different event formats.

The centre also offers audiovisual equipment, Wi-Fi, event coordination and catering services.

Another major advantage is its location alongside Mövenpick Hotel Windhoek and Mercure Hotel Windhoek, allowing organisers to combine conference facilities and accommodation in one integrated event environment.

Why choose WICC?

  • Excellent for large delegate numbers
  • Multiple breakout and meeting rooms
  • Exhibition capability
  • Flexible conference configurations
  • On-site event support
  • Adjacent hotel accommodation
  • Suitable for national and international events

Ideal for: International associations, government conferences, corporate conventions, exhibitions and large-scale business events.

2. Hilton Windhoek: Windhoek

Hilton Windhoek

Best for: Corporate meetings, executive events and conferences requiring a central city location

For planners looking for an established international hotel environment in the heart of Windhoek, Hilton Windhoek is a strong option.

Its central location makes it particularly convenient for corporate delegates who want access to the city's business district, restaurants and other amenities.

The hotel provides meeting and event facilities alongside accommodation, making it suitable for organisations that prefer to keep delegates in one property.

For international planners, the appeal is straightforward:

Accommodation, meetings, dining and delegate services under one roof.

Why choose Hilton Windhoek?

  • Central Windhoek location
  • Integrated accommodation and conference facilities
  • Suitable for corporate meetings
  • Appropriate for executive-level events
  • Recognisable international hotel brand

Ideal for: Corporate meetings, executive conferences, workshops, association meetings and international business groups.

3. Windhoek Country Club Resort: Windhoek

Windhoek Country Club Resort

Best for: Corporate conferences, incentives, gala events and residential meetings

Located close to Windhoek's international airport corridor, Windhoek Country Club Resort offers a resort-style alternative to a traditional city conference hotel.

The property combines accommodation, meeting facilities, dining and leisure amenities, allowing organisers to create a more relaxed environment for delegates.

This can be particularly valuable for conferences where the experience outside the meeting room is just as important as the conference itself.

Why choose Windhoek Country Club Resort?

  • Resort environment
  • Conference and meeting facilities
  • Accommodation on site
  • Suitable for corporate groups
  • Leisure and entertainment options
  • Convenient for combining business and relaxation

Ideal for: Corporate conferences, incentive groups, executive retreats, gala dinners and residential meetings.

4. Avani Windhoek Hotel & Casino: Windhoek

Avani Windhoek Hotel & Casino

Best for: Corporate meetings, conferences, networking events and city-based programmes

Located on Independence Avenue in central Windhoek, Avani Windhoek Hotel & Casino provides a convenient city-centre option for meetings and corporate events.

The property offers multiple meeting spaces and accommodation, making it suitable for organisations looking for a central business-events base.

Its location is particularly useful when delegates need easy access to Windhoek's CBD and surrounding business environment.

Why choose Avani Windhoek?

  • Central location
  • Multiple meeting spaces
  • On-site accommodation
  • Suitable for corporate events
  • Convenient for city-based delegate programmes

Ideal for: Corporate meetings, workshops, seminars, conferences and networking events.

5. MTC Dome: Swakopmund

Gecko Ridge

Note: The venue itself is the MTC Dome Namibia, rather than Gecko Ridge.

Best for: Conferences, exhibitions, corporate events and large gatherings

Swakopmund offers one of Namibia's most distinctive conference environments, and the MTC Dome takes that proposition a step further.

The conference centre features six meeting rooms, with the main Swakop room accommodating up to 600 people in cinema-style seating and 350 in banquet-style seating.

The venue also has breakaway areas, exhibition space and boardrooms, with some offering views towards the Atlantic Ocean. An outdoor rooftop deck can accommodate up to 350 people and includes a bar and stage.

The wider complex also includes a hotel, catering facilities, sports and wellness facilities, making it possible to combine business, accommodation and leisure.

Why choose the MTC Dome?

  • Up to 600 delegates in the main conference room
  • Six meeting rooms
  • Exhibition and breakaway areas
  • Rooftop event space
  • On-site hotel
  • Catering facilities
  • Coastal Swakopmund setting

Ideal for: Conferences, corporate events, exhibitions, product launches, seminars and incentive programmes.

6. Strand Hotel Swakopmund: Swakopmund

Best for: Corporate meetings, executive events and conferences with a coastal setting

Few Namibian conference destinations offer the atmosphere of Swakopmund.

The Strand Hotel places delegates directly within one of Namibia's most recognisable coastal destinations, combining business facilities with accommodation, restaurants and access to the town's attractions.

It is particularly appealing for organisers who want delegates to experience Namibia beyond the conference room.

A meeting at the Strand can be combined with a coastal programme, desert excursion, networking dinner or incentive activity.

Why choose Strand Hotel Swakopmund?

  • Prime coastal setting
  • On-site accommodation
  • Meeting facilities
  • Restaurants and leisure options
  • Strong destination appeal
  • Excellent base for incentive activities

Ideal for: Executive meetings, smaller conferences, incentive programmes, corporate retreats and destination events.

7. Protea Hotel by Marriott Walvis Bay Indongo: Walvis Bay

Best for: Corporate conferences, meetings and coastal business events

For organisers considering Walvis Bay, the Protea Hotel by Marriott Walvis Bay Indongo is one of the strongest conference options in the town.

The property has four event rooms and approximately 375 square metres of total event space. Its largest conference room can accommodate up to 280 people in theatre style, while smaller rooms provide useful options for breakaway sessions.

The venue also provides audiovisual equipment, Wi-Fi and spaces for networking before and after sessions.

Its location makes it particularly attractive for conferences that combine business with the Atlantic coast, desert landscapes and marine experiences of the Erongo region.

Why choose Protea Hotel Walvis Bay Indongo?

  • Four event rooms
  • Up to 280 delegates in the largest space
  • Breakout facilities
  • On-site accommodation
  • Audiovisual facilities
  • Wi-Fi
  • Strong location for coastal programmes

Ideal for: Corporate conferences, workshops, business meetings, association events and incentive groups.

8. Protea Hotel by Marriott Walvis Bay Pelican Bay: Walvis Bay

Best for: Smaller conferences, corporate meetings and intimate business events

For smaller groups, Protea Hotel Walvis Bay Pelican Bay provides a more intimate alternative in Walvis Bay.

The hotel offers meeting facilities alongside accommodation, making it suitable for organisations that do not require a large convention centre.

Its location also makes it possible to incorporate Walvis Bay's marine and desert attractions into the wider event programme.

Why choose Pelican Bay?

  • Convenient Walvis Bay location
  • Accommodation on site
  • Meeting facilities
  • Suitable for smaller groups
  • Easy access to coastal experiences

Ideal for: Smaller conferences, executive meetings, workshops, corporate retreats and incentive groups.

9. Droombos: Windhoek

Droombos

Best for: Executive retreats, private meetings, workshops and intimate events

If your event requires something more personal than a large conference centre, Droombos offers a different type of meeting environment.

Situated outside the intensity of the central business district, the property provides a more private and relaxed setting.

This makes it particularly attractive for:

  • Executive retreats
  • Strategy sessions
  • Leadership meetings
  • Workshops
  • Private corporate dinners
  • Small conferences

The setting can help create an environment where delegates can focus, engage and network away from the conventional hotel conference environment.

Why choose Droombos?

  • Private setting
  • Suitable for executive groups
  • Conference facilities
  • Accommodation
  • Outdoor event possibilities
  • Strong option for destination-style meetings

Ideal for: Executive retreats, leadership meetings, strategy sessions and intimate corporate events.

10. Midgard Country Estate: Near Windhoek

Best for: Corporate retreats, residential conferences and incentive programmes

For organisers wanting to take delegates away from the city altogether, Midgard Country Estate offers a country-retreat approach to business events.

Located outside Windhoek, the estate combines accommodation, conference facilities and recreational activities in a countryside environment.

The property has previously been used for conferences and stakeholder events, and its wider facilities include restaurants, recreational amenities, event spaces and conference facilities.

This makes Midgard particularly interesting for organisations looking to combine strategic business sessions with team-building, relaxation and outdoor experiences.

Why choose Midgard?

  • Country-estate environment
  • Accommodation
  • Conference facilities
  • Recreational activities
  • Suitable for residential programmes
  • Strong team-building potential
  • Away-from-the-city atmosphere

Ideal for: Leadership retreats, strategy sessions, residential conferences, team-building programmes and incentive groups.

How to Choose the Right Conference Venue in Namibia

The "best" conference venue depends entirely on the type of event you are organising.

A venue suitable for 1,500 delegates will not necessarily be the right choice for a 20-person executive retreat.

Before making a decision, consider the following.

Delegate Numbers

Start with your expected number of delegates.

Then consider whether the venue can accommodate:

  • Plenary sessions
  • Breakout sessions
  • Networking
  • Catering
  • Exhibitions
  • Registration
  • VIP areas

Do not plan solely around the maximum capacity.

Comfort matters.

Accommodation

If your delegates are travelling from outside the destination, accommodation becomes a major consideration.

Ideally, the venue should either have accommodation on site or be close to suitable hotels.

For larger events, consider whether there are enough rooms across several properties within a reasonable distance.

Location

Ask yourself what experience you want delegates to have.

Want convenience?

Choose Windhoek.

Want a coastal experience?

Consider Swakopmund or Walvis Bay.

Want a retreat?

Look at properties outside the main city centres.

Want wildlife and nature?

Consider a lodge-based conference or a conference combined with a safari extension.

Conference Venue vs Hotel: Which Is Better?

There is no universal answer.

Choose a dedicated conference centre when:

  • You have a large delegate number
  • You require exhibition space
  • You need multiple breakout rooms
  • You require large plenary facilities
  • You are hosting an association or international conference

Choose a hotel when:

  • Accommodation is a priority
  • You have a small or medium-sized group
  • You want everything under one roof
  • Convenience is important

Choose a lodge or country estate when:

  • The event is highly experiential
  • You are hosting senior executives
  • Privacy is important
  • Team-building is part of the programme
  • You want delegates to disconnect from the city

Make the Venue Part of the Experience

One of Namibia's biggest advantages as a MICE destination is that the conference does not have to end when the meeting ends.

A conference in Windhoek can be followed by a desert excursion.

A corporate meeting in Swakopmund can include an Atlantic cruise.

A Walvis Bay event can incorporate marine and desert experiences.

A leadership retreat can be combined with a lodge or wildlife experience.

This is where Namibia can outperform a conventional conference destination.

Do not choose a venue in isolation. Choose a venue that supports the story you want your event to tell.

What Should You Ask a Venue Before Booking?

Before signing a contract, ask the venue for detailed information on:

Capacity

  • Maximum theatre capacity
  • Classroom capacity
  • Banquet capacity
  • Boardroom capacity
  • Breakout-room availability

Technology

  • Wi-Fi
  • Projectors
  • Screens
  • Sound systems
  • Microphones
  • Hybrid-event capabilities
  • Technical support

Catering

  • Conference packages
  • Coffee breaks
  • Lunch options
  • Gala dinners
  • Dietary requirements
  • Beverage packages

Accommodation

  • Number of rooms
  • Room categories
  • Group rates
  • Check-in arrangements
  • Early arrival and late departure options

Logistics

  • Parking
  • Airport transfers
  • Delegate transport
  • Supplier access
  • Loading areas
  • Security
  • Accessibility

Event Support

  • Dedicated conference coordinator
  • On-site technical team
  • Registration support
  • Event staffing
  • Emergency procedures

When Should You Book a Conference Venue in Namibia?

For international conferences and larger events, early planning is strongly recommended.

Ideally, begin the venue-selection process 12–24 months before the event, particularly if you require:

  • Large accommodation blocks
  • International speakers
  • Multiple venues
  • Exhibition space
  • Complex transport
  • International delegates
  • Visa support
  • Extensive event production

Smaller corporate meetings may require considerably less lead time, but availability should always be checked before confirming dates with delegates.

Beyond the Conference Room: What Can Delegates Do?

One of the biggest reasons to host an event in Namibia is the opportunity to turn a business trip into a destination experience.

Depending on the location and programme, delegates can experience:

Windhoek

  • City tours
  • Cultural experiences
  • Restaurants
  • Craft markets
  • Corporate networking events

Swakopmund

  • Desert excursions
  • Sandboarding
  • Quad biking
  • Marine cruises
  • Scenic flights
  • Adventure activities

Walvis Bay

  • Marine excursions
  • Desert experiences
  • Coastal activities
  • Dune adventures

Beyond the Cities

  • Wildlife experiences
  • Conservation activities
  • Luxury lodge stays
  • Desert landscapes
  • Cultural experiences

This creates a powerful opportunity for pre- and post-conference extensions.

Final Thoughts: Choosing the Right Venue in Namibia

Namibia's conference-venue landscape is becoming increasingly diverse.

From the scale of the Windhoek International Convention Centre to the coastal character of Swakopmund, the business environment of Walvis Bay and the tranquillity of country estates and lodges, planners have a growing selection of options.

But choosing a venue should never be based on capacity alone.

The right venue should answer three questions:

Can it deliver the event?

Can it accommodate the delegates?

Can it contribute to the experience?

When the answer to all three is yes, you have more than a conference venue.

You have the foundation for a successful event.

Planning a Conference in Namibia?

Whether you are organising a large international conference, corporate meeting, executive retreat, association event, incentive programme or private business gathering, selecting the right venue is only the beginning.

The real value comes from connecting the venue with the right accommodation, transport, activities, suppliers and destination experiences.

In Namibia, the meeting may take place inside the conference room, but the experience begins the moment your delegates arrive.
