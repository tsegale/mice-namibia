The Westin Cape Town

Cape Town, South Africa

The Westin Cape Town provides well-appointed conference facilities in a full-service hotel setting, suited to corporate meetings, conferences and incentive groups travelling into South Africa.

With on-site dining, a spa and comfortable accommodation alongside its event space, it offers a convenient single-venue solution for multi-day business events.
