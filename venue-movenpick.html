About This Venue

Movenpick offers a polished hotel setting suited to corporate meetings, small conferences and incentive groups travelling through Namibia. Guests have access to well-appointed function space, on-site dining and outdoor areas that make for relaxed networking breaks between sessions.

Its combination of comfortable accommodation and flexible event space makes it a dependable choice for organisations looking to pair business sessions with a high standard of hospitality.
