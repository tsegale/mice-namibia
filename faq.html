FAQ

MICE Frequently Asked Questions - Africa

Answers to the questions we hear most often from organisations planning meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions across Africa.

What does MICE stand for?

MICE stands for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions. It covers corporate events, business meetings, incentive travel, conferences and related group programmes.

Which African destinations do you offer for MICE travel?

We offer MICE programmes across Botswana, Kenya, Namibia, Rwanda, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

Can you create a tailor-made MICE programme?

Yes. We design customised programmes based on your group size, objectives, budget, preferred destination, dates and interests.

Can you organise the entire MICE programme?

Yes. We can assist with accommodation, conference venues, airport transfers, transportation, meals, activities, excursions, team-building and other event logistics.

Can business events be combined with leisure or safari experiences?

Absolutely. Africa offers excellent opportunities to combine meetings and conferences with safaris, wildlife, cultural experiences, adventure and leisure extensions.

Why choose Namibia for a MICE event?

Namibia offers a unique combination of modern business facilities, spectacular landscapes, wildlife and memorable incentive experiences. It is particularly suitable for companies looking to combine business with an authentic African experience.

What MICE destinations can we visit in Namibia?

Popular options include Windhoek, Swakopmund, Walvis Bay, Sossusvlei and the Etosha region, depending on the objectives and length of the programme.

Can you organise a conference and safari in Namibia?

Yes. A corporate programme can combine a conference or meeting in Windhoek with safari and leisure experiences in other parts of Namibia.

Why is Botswana suitable for incentive travel?

Botswana is renowned for its exceptional wildlife, luxury safari lodges and wilderness experiences, making it an excellent destination for memorable incentive programmes.

Can you arrange a safari-based corporate incentive in Botswana?

Yes. We can design programmes incorporating luxury accommodation, safari experiences, group activities and special dining experiences.

Why choose Kenya for MICE travel?

Kenya offers a strong combination of business facilities, international connectivity, wildlife and coastal experiences, making it suitable for both conferences and incentive travel.

Can a Kenya conference be combined with a safari?

Yes. Groups can combine business events with safari experiences and other leisure activities, creating a balanced corporate programme.

Why is Rwanda a good MICE destination?

Rwanda offers a modern and organised environment for business events, particularly in Kigali, while also providing opportunities for cultural, nature and incentive experiences.

What MICE experiences can we include in Rwanda?

Depending on the programme, groups can enjoy Kigali experiences, cultural activities, nature-based excursions and incentive experiences.

Why choose South Africa for a MICE event?

South Africa offers a wide range of international conference venues, hotels, restaurants, attractions and business infrastructure, making it suitable for events of different sizes and requirements.

Which South African destinations are suitable for MICE?

Depending on the programme, popular options include Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban and the surrounding safari regions.

Can you arrange a South African conference combined with a safari?

Yes. Corporate groups can combine meetings or conferences with safari, sightseeing, wine, culinary and other leisure experiences.

Why choose Tanzania for incentive travel?

Tanzania provides outstanding opportunities to combine business travel with wildlife, safari and beach experiences, making it an attractive incentive destination.

Can you combine a conference with a safari in Tanzania?

Yes. Programmes can be designed around meetings or conferences followed by safari experiences, with options to extend the trip to Zanzibar.

Why consider Uganda for MICE travel?

Uganda offers a combination of business facilities, nature, wildlife and unique experiences, providing opportunities for companies looking for something different.

What incentive experiences are available in Uganda?

Depending on the itinerary, groups can enjoy wildlife experiences, nature activities, cultural encounters and adventure programmes.

Why choose Zambia for an incentive programme?

Zambia is well suited to incentive travel thanks to its wildlife, natural attractions, adventure activities and authentic safari experiences.

Can you combine a Zambia business event with Victoria Falls?

Yes. Victoria Falls can be incorporated into a corporate or incentive programme, providing an unforgettable experience for delegates.

Why is Zimbabwe suitable for MICE and incentive travel?

Zimbabwe offers spectacular natural attractions, wildlife and cultural experiences, with Victoria Falls being a major attraction for incentive groups.

Can you organise a corporate programme around Victoria Falls?

Yes. We can create programmes combining corporate activities, accommodation, sightseeing, adventure and Victoria Falls experiences.

How far in advance should we book a MICE programme?

We recommend booking as early as possible, particularly for larger groups, peak travel periods and major conferences, to secure suitable venues, accommodation and transportation.

Can you accommodate both small and large corporate groups?

Yes. We can assist with programmes for small executive groups, medium-sized corporate groups and larger delegations, depending on the destination and requirements.

Can you arrange airport transfers and transportation?

Yes. We can coordinate airport transfers, private vehicles, coaches, shuttles and transportation between destinations and activities.

Can you arrange accommodation and conference venues?

Yes. We can source suitable hotels, lodges, resorts and conference venues according to your group size, location, event requirements and budget.

How do we request a MICE proposal?

Contact us with your destination, preferred dates, number of delegates, type of event, accommodation requirements and any activities you would like to include. We will use this information to develop a tailor-made proposal for your group.
